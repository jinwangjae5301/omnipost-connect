OmniPost

OmniPost is a private desktop publishing tool. The owner runs it on their own PC to schedule and upload their own marketing videos and posts to the YouTube channels, Facebook pages, Instagram and Threads accounts they own, and to add a comment with the product link under each post.

No data leaves the owner's PC except calls to each platform's official API. There are no other users.

OmniPost는 운영자 본인 PC에서 실행되는 업로드 도구로, 본인 소유의 YouTube 채널, Facebook 페이지, Instagram·Threads 계정에 본인 마케팅 콘텐츠를 예약·업로드하고 상품 링크 댓글을 다는 데 사용됩니다.

YouTube
