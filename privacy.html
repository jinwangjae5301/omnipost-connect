OmniPost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

1. What OmniPost is

OmniPost is a desktop program that the owner runs on their own PC to publish their own marketing content (videos, captions, comments) to social media accounts they own or manage: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Threads. OmniPost has no server and no other users. All data stays on the owner's PC.

2. Data we access

  • YouTube (Google) data: channel ID and channel name, permission to upload videos, set video titles/descriptions/tags/privacy, post comments on the owner's own videos, and read basic channel/video statistics (views, likes).
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) data: page/account ID and name, permission to publish posts, reels and comments, and read basic post insights.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens issued by these platforms.

3. How we use the data

  • Only to upload the owner's content to the owner's own accounts, post the owner's comments (such as a product link), and show the owner how the posts perform.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or training AI models.
  • OmniPost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and security

  • Tokens and account IDs are stored only in a local file on the owner's PC with owner-only file permissions. They are never sent to any server other than the platform's own official API.
  • Uploaded video files remain in the owner's local folders.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept only while the account is connected. Removing an account in OmniPost deletes its stored tokens from the PC.
  • You can revoke OmniPost's access at any time: Google: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, Meta: Facebook/Instagram Settings → Apps and websites.

6. YouTube

OmniPost uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
